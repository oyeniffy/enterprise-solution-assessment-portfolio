## Engagement Context

A commercial banking client engaged Task Systems Limited to evaluate its current IT infrastructure and Microsoft software licensing posture against its Volume Licensing pool — with the objective of identifying compliance risk, security vulnerabilities, and optimization opportunities across both its on-premises estate and its Microsoft 365 environment.

## Objectives

1. **Purpose** — establish a factual, evidence-based understanding of the bank's current licensing and infrastructure posture.
2. **Core objectives** — determine license compliance status, identify security and operational risk exposure, and surface cost/optimization opportunities across both on-premises and cloud-hosted Microsoft workloads.
3. **Scope** — covered on-premises server and RDS licensing, SQL Server core licensing, and the Microsoft 365 tenant (license pool vs. assignment).
4. **Data sources** — structured exports and discovery data covering discovered infrastructure inventory, license consumption records, and Microsoft 365 tenant assignment data.

## Methodology

The assessment followed a consistent two-tier structure, applied separately to the on-premises estate and the Microsoft 365 environment:

1. **Discovered infrastructure inventory** — a consolidated, tool-assisted discovery pass across servers and workloads to establish an authoritative deployment baseline.
2. **Licensing analysis** — reconciliation of discovered deployments (Windows Server & RDS, SQL Server cores, Microsoft 365 user allocations) against the client's actual license pool and entitlement position.
3. **Security & operational risk review** — assessment of risk arising from the discovered infrastructure and licensing position, feeding into a single consolidated, prioritized risk register.

This produced a **Consolidated Risk Register**, prioritized by severity and urgency, which in turn drove a structured, time-phased recommendations framework:

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| Immediate compliance true-up | Correcting licensing misalignment with the greatest audit/financial exposure |
| Urgent security remediation | Addressing the highest-severity security and operational risk items |
| Operational stabilization & clean-up | Resolving inventory, visibility, and process gaps |
| Strategic enhancement | Medium-term modernization and architecture improvements |
| Continuous optimization & governance | Ongoing licensing governance to prevent future drift |

## Categories of Findings

Findings spanned two domains — on-premises infrastructure (discovered server/RDS estate, SQL Server core licensing exposure, and associated security/operational risk) and the Microsoft 365 environment (discovered user allocation patterns and licensing-pool-vs-assignment analysis) — each rolled into the consolidated risk register described above. ## Value Delivered

- A single, evidence-based view of licensing compliance across both on-premises and Microsoft 365 environments, replacing fragmented departmental visibility
- A prioritized risk register giving executive stakeholders a clear, sequenced basis for remediation investment
- A phased action plan — from immediate compliance correction through to continuous governance — designed to prevent recurrence of the same gaps
- Clear identification of security and operational risk exposure tied directly to licensing and infrastructure findings, not treated as a separate workstream
